The Bitwage Partner API covers two products. Each product has its own
endpoints. Use one set or the other for a given person. A user created on one
side is not a recipient on the other.

| Product | Who it is for | Endpoints | Start here |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bitwage Business** | A company pays its contractors, employees, and vendors. | `/api/company` and `/api/companies` | [Integration lifecycle](/guides/partner-integration-lifecycle) |
| **Bitwage Individual** | A person receives payments from their own employers or clients, inside your UI. | `/api/user` | [Private label](/guides/private-label) |

Bitwage Business also covers onboarding client companies and paying them
through, and crypto checkout. Bitwage Individual is EUR payers only.

## Environments

| Environment | API Base URL | App URL |
|---|---|---|
| Sandbox | `https://api.sandbox.bitwage.com` | `https://app.sandbox.bitwage.com` |
| Production | `https://api2.bitwage.com` | `https://app.bitwage.com` |

Use the sandbox environment for development and testing before going to production.
