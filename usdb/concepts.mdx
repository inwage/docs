## USDb and its backing

USDb is a USD-pegged stablecoin built for businesses, issued on the
**Rootstock** Bitcoin sidechain and backed 1:1 by US dollar reserves.

Through the USDb API, you fund and redeem USDb using **USD**: minting converts
a company's USD into USDb, and burning converts USDb back into USD. USD is the
settlement asset for these conversions.

## Wallets and rails

A wallet (also called a balance account) holds a single currency. Crypto
wallets also have a network rail. For USDb flows, a company uses two wallets:

| Currency | Rail | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `USD` | — | The settlement asset you mint from and burn to. |
| `USDB` | `ROOTSTOCK` | The stablecoin you mint to and burn from. |

A crypto wallet requires a `rail`, so request the USDb wallet on `ROOTSTOCK`.
A `USD` wallet is a fiat balance account and takes no rail.

## Eligibility

Before a company can mint or burn, it must:

- Have completed full KYC.
- Have an **active** USD wallet.
- Have an **active** USDb wallet on the `ROOTSTOCK` rail.

If any requirement is missing, mint and burn requests are rejected.

## Amounts

Amounts are always expressed in **major units** as a string — for example
`"100.00"` for 100 USDb. The minimum amount for a mint or burn is **10**.

## Conversions are asynchronous

Mint, burn, and withdrawal requests are processed asynchronously. The API
acknowledges the request immediately, then settles the conversion in the
background. Use [webhooks](/usdb/webhooks) to learn when a flow reaches a
terminal status rather than polling.

<Note>
Because conversions settle in the background, a successful mint or burn
response only confirms that the request was **accepted**, not that funds have
moved. Wait for the corresponding `wallet.mint` or `wallet.burn` webhook
before treating a conversion as complete.
</Note>
