USDb is a USD-pegged stablecoin built for businesses, issued on the
[Rootstock](https://rootstock.io) Bitcoin sidechain and backed 1:1 by US
dollar reserves. It's designed to operate inside enterprise financial
workflows — payouts, payroll, and treasury — rather than as a standalone
trading asset. The USDb API lets you convert a company's USD into USDb and
back, and manage the wallets that hold them.

## Why USDb

USDb brings the speed and reach of a blockchain stablecoin to your treasury,
while staying anchored to the US dollar:

- **Dollar-pegged** — backed 1:1 by US dollar reserves.
- **Built for businesses** — designed for payouts, payroll, and treasury, not just trading.
- **Built on Bitcoin infrastructure** — issued on Rootstock for fast, low-cost settlement.
- **Always available** — mint, burn, and move funds 24/7.
- **Fully managed** — Bitwage custodies the wallets and sponsors network fees.

## What you can do

- **Request wallets** for USD and USDb (Rootstock).
- **Mint USDb** by converting a company's USD into USDb.
- **Burn USDb** to convert it back into USD.
- **Withdraw** crypto from a wallet to an external address.
- **Track activity** through wallet transactions and webhooks.

## Authentication

All USDb endpoints support **API key authentication only** — OAuth 2.0 bearer
tokens are not accepted. See the [Authentication page](/usdb/authentication)
for how to obtain and use your API key.
